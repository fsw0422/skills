Create one file per normalized company when identity is unambiguous. Use a safe lowercase filename, for example acme.md. Preserve existing user-authored content and add one role section per canonical LinkedIn URL.

## 3. Sources

Add dated official sources first, then reputable reporting and clearly labeled Reddit, Blind, Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, and other community evidence.

## Authoring rules

- Research company facts once and reuse them across role sections.
- Give every material current fact a source and access date.
- Use official sources first; label estimates and anonymous anecdotes.
- Store the full classification rationale here.
- Use exactly A — Great Fit, B — Normal Fit, Investigate, or Skip.
- Do not add Application strategy or Questions to ask sections.
- Remove every unused placeholder before completion.
- Keep application timelines in tracker.csv, not this file.
- Do not store credentials, demographic answers, confidential work-system text, or unnecessary personal data.
